# 🚁 Welcome to UHS-UAVHardwareStack

Welcome to **UHS-UAVHardwareStack**! We are an open-source hardware initiative dedicated to designing and building UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicle) hardware completely **from scratch**. 

Our core philosophy is simple: break down the complexities of drone hardware, share our journey, and empower everyone—from beginners to experts—to learn the basics and build their own flight systems.

---

## 🧩 The Core Concept: Modular Hardware Stacks

Forget rigid, all-in-one boards. Our hardware architecture is designed around **independent, stackable modules**. 

This modular approach allows you to:
*   **Customize:** Stack only the specific modules you need for your mission.
*   **Upgrade Easily:** Swap out a single module (like a sensor board or a communication link) without needing to replace the entire flight controller.
*   **Learn Step-by-Step:** Understand how a UAV works by building and testing one module at a time.

## 🚀 Current Hardware Stack Ideas

We are currently developing two primary hardware stacks, engineered from the ground up:

### 1. The Standard FC (Flight Controller) Stack
A fundamental, robust flight controller built from scratch. This stack focuses on the core necessities of UAV flight, providing a perfect learning platform for those who want to understand exactly how drone stabilization and motor control work at the bare-metal level.

### 2. The IoT Gateway FC Stack
Taking the traditional flight controller to the next level. This stack combines standard FC capabilities with an integrated IoT Gateway. It is designed for advanced connectivity, long-range telemetry, and seamless integration with cloud platforms and external networks.

## 📚 Learn and Build With Us

Everything in this organization is built on the principle of **open education**. 

*   **From Scratch:** We don't just assemble off-the-shelf parts; we design the schematics, route the PCBs, and write the firmware. 
*   **Fully Open Source:** All of our design files, schematics, and source code are available for you to study, modify, and manufacture.
*   **Beginner Friendly:** We aim to document our process thoroughly so you can follow along, learn the fundamental physics and electronics behind UAVs, and eventually build your own stack.

## 🤝 How to Get Involved

Whether you are an electronics hobbyist, an embedded software engineer, or someone who just loves drones, we want you to build with us!

*   **Explore our Repositories:** Check out our current schematics, PCB designs, and firmware projects.
*   **Read the Documentation:** Dive into our wikis and guides to learn the basics of UAV hardware design.
*   **Contribute:** Found a bug in a schematic? Have an idea for a new stackable module? Open an issue or submit a pull request!

Let's stack, build, and fly! 🚁✨
